Questions

How do I lock the screen rotation?

Tap the floating lock once. Your screen stays exactly as it is. Tap again to unlock.

Can I move the lock?

Yes. Hold the lock and slide it wherever you want it on the screen.

What does the Quick Menu do?

Two taps open it. It keeps the most used buttons right under your thumb, in a design you pick.

Does it cost anything?

No. Lock 'n Rota is free. Support is optional, via Ko-fi below.

Which Android versions are supported?

Current Android phones. Install it from Google Play and it picks the right version.
